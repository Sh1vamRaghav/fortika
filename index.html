fortika

A small private keepsake app for two people: a wall of pinned polaroid photos and a wall of question-and-answer memories.

What it does

  • Photo wall. Choose a photo, crop it to a square, add a caption and a date, and pin it to the wall. Tap a pinned photo to enlarge it, flip it over to see the date, or spin it.
  • Q&A wall. Pin a question and add a reply from each person, like a shared notebook of little memories.
  • Works offline. Photos and notes appear on your phone right away and sync when you are back online.

How it uses Google Drive

fortika has no server of its own. Everything you add is stored in a folder named “MemoryWalls” in the Google Drive account of the person who set the app up. It asks for the drive.file permission, which lets the app see only the files and folders it created itself. It cannot read anything else in that Drive.

Who it is for

fortika is not a public service. It is installed by the two people it was made for, and there is no sign-up.
