Privacy policy

fortika · last updated September 20, 2026

fortika is a private photo and memory app used by two people. This page explains what data it touches and what happens to it.

What fortika accesses

fortika connects to one Google Drive account, the one belonging to the person who set the app up, using the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. That permission only allows the app to see files and folders that fortika itself created. It cannot see, list, or read any other file in the Drive.

What is stored, and where

  • The photos you add (cropped to a square), their captions and dates.
  • The questions and replies you add on the Q&A wall.

This content is stored in a folder called “MemoryWalls” in that Google Drive account, and a copy is kept on your own phone so the app works offline. There is no fortika server, no database, no analytics, and no advertising.

How the data is used

Only to show your photos and notes back to you and to keep the two phones in sync. It is never used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Sharing

fortika does not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. The content is visible to the people who use the app and to the owner of the Google Drive account it is stored in, because it lives in that Drive.

Google API Services User Data Policy

fortika's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete a photo or Q&A card inside the app (press and hold it) and it is removed from the wall.
  • Delete the “MemoryWalls” folder in Google Drive to remove everything fortika stored.
  • Revoke the app's access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the copy kept on your phone.

Children

fortika is not directed at children and is not offered to the general public.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change too.
